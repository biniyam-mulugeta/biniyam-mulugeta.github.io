01 / About

Researcher & Engineer

I am a PhD candidate and research assistant at the University of Debrecen, Hungary, where I develop AI systems that bridge the gap between clinical data complexity and machine interpretability.

My doctoral work spans two complementary tracks: deep learning for medical imaging diagnostics — building end-to-end pipelines that process histopathology, radiology, and clinical tabular data — and agentic AI systems designed to automate multi-step reasoning workflows.

I design architectures integrating Apache Kafka for real-time event streaming, RAG-based knowledge retrieval, neuro-symbolic microservices, and Human-in-the-Loop orchestration to create reproducible, auditable intelligent pipelines.

Neuro-Symbolic IDS for Apache Log Anomaly Detection

Developing an intrusion detection system that combines deep neural anomaly scoring with symbolic temporal logic reasoning. The system maps detected threats to MITRE ATT&CK entries via OWL ontologies and applies LTL-based multi-stage attack reasoning on the CICIDS 2017 dataset.
