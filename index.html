About Me

I am a researcher at the University of Debrecen, Hungary, working under the supervision of Prof. András Hajdu in the Faculty of Informatics. My work focuses on deep learning architectures for medical imaging — designing novel hybrid models for disease classification, anatomical segmentation, and diagnostic decision support.

Previously I served as a Lecturer and Researcher at the University of Gondar, Ethiopia (2022–2025), where I led research projects on AI for medical diagnostics and agricultural disease detection, producing a substantial body of peer-reviewed work across IEEE, Elsevier, Wiley, and Springer journals.

My current research directions include Multi-Modal Vision-Language Models for cross-modality medical imaging, frequency-aware 3D brain tumour segmentation, knowledge-graph-augmented RAG systems for plant pathology QA, and systematic reviews of foundation models in healthcare AI.

02

Research Interests

*

Medical Image Analysis

Novel deep learning architectures for classifying and detecting diseases from radiology, endoscopy, histopathology, and X-ray images — spinal, gastrointestinal, pulmonary, and oncological conditions.

*

Computer Vision

Hybrid CNN-Transformer models, involutional neural networks, wavelet-based feature extraction, and attention mechanisms for high-accuracy visual recognition in medical and agricultural settings.

*

Vision-Language Models

Multi-modal VLM architectures bridging imaging and clinical text for automated diagnostic reporting, cross-modality reasoning, and EHR-integrated clinical decision support.

*

Computational Pathology

Context-aware spectral gating and frequency-aware methods for domain-robust histopathology image classification using Vision Transformers for whole-slide image analysis.

*

3D Volumetric Segmentation

Frequency-aware text-guided 3D segmentation with hierarchical semantic decomposition and contrastive alignment for brain tumour delineation in volumetric MRI data.

*

Generative AI & RAG Systems

Knowledge-graph-augmented RAG architectures for clinical and agricultural QA; generative AI for medical data synthesis and systematic literature review automation.
